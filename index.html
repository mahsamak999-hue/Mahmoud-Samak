    The designer

    المصمم

    I turn ideas into bold visual stories. Cairo-based, bilingual by default, and happiest where a brand meets a headline.

    I graduated from Badr University in Cairo (BUC) in 2023 with an A- on a graduation project in branding and visual identity systems, and hold a professional diploma in graphic design from Adobe (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign). Since then I've worked across agencies on campaigns for some of the region's biggest names, blending craft with precision, and lately with AI.
